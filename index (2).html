🔒 Privacy Policy

Who we are

Math Adventure is developed by Suresh Thiagarajah. Designed for children aged 6–12 to practise maths skills.

Data we collect

Math Adventure collects no personal data. The only information stored is your optional leaderboard name and score, saved locally on your device. This never leaves your device and is never sent to any server.

Children's privacy (COPPA / GDPR-K)

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. No accounts, emails, or identifiable information are required. The leaderboard name is stored only on your device.

Advertising & tracking

Math Adventure contains no advertising, no in-app purchases, and no third-party tracking of any kind.

Data deletion

Delete all stored scores at any time using "Clear all scores" on the Scores screen.

Contact

Privacy questions: suresh@mathadventure.app
